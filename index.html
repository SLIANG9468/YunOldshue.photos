章运（Yun Oldshue) BELIEVES THAT EVERY PHOTOGRAPH HAS THE POWER TO PRESERVE A MOMENT IN TIME.

Yun has been volunteering as an event photographer for many years, capturing community events such as running races, triathlons, basketball games, family celebrations, gatherings with friends, and music concerts. Over the past four years, Yun has also worked as a professional photographer. As a member of Purple Photos Group, Yun has photographed a wide variety of events throughout the Chicagoland area. Yun's journey from volunteer photographer to professional has reinforced Yun's belief that photography has always been more than just a job—it is a passion and a way of preserving life’s meaningful moments. Yun believes that every photograph has the power to preserve a moment in time, creating a lasting collection of memories that becomes part of our shared history.

Yun's professional focus is event photography. Over the past four years with Purple Photos Group, Yun has photographed conferences, corporate celebrations, golf outings, graduations, and professional headshots for clients throughout the Chicagoland area. Yun enjoys capturing genuine emotions and the special moments that make every event unique.

Outside of Yun's professional work, Yun is passionate about wildlife and landscape photography. Through Yun's lens, Yun hopes to help people appreciate the beauty of the world around them while documenting meaningful moments—both big and small—that connect people with their loved ones.

Friends and clients describe Yun as easy-going, approachable, and genuinely enjoyable to work with. Whether Yun is capturing the excitement of a special event or the unique character of a wild animal, Yun strives to present every subject with authenticity and respect, allowing its true story to shine through.
